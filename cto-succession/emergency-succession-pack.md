## 6. Holding messages

### To the technology team

[Name] will be away from work for a period. [Interim leader] is leading technology in the meantime, with the full support of [CEO]. Our priorities and commitments are unchanged. Please bring anything you would have taken to [name] to [interim leader].

### To key customers

We want to let you know that [interim leader] is leading our technology organization for a period. Your account team and delivery commitments are unchanged, and [interim leader] is available at [contact] if you would like to speak.

### To the board

Facts as known, interim leader named, commitments at risk (if any), next update date.

## 7. First 48 hours checklist

- [ ] CEO confirms interim leader and signs authority letter
- [ ] Board chair informed the same day
- [ ] Team message sent within 24 hours
- [ ] Access holders confirmed reachable
- [ ] Top ten commitments reviewed with owners
- [ ] Key customers and vendors contacted per relationship map
- [ ] Next review of the situation scheduled
