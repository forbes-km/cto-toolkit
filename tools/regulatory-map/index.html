Regulatory Map for Technology Leaders

Data Governance covers privacy and AI Governance covers AI; nothing ties the regimes together or says who owns which clock. This page does. It is organized by what each regime regulates, because that is how ownership falls out, and it ends with the mistake that turns a manageable set of obligations into six parallel processes.

This is a map, not advice. Dates here were checked against primary and legal-firm sources in September 2026, and several regimes were still moving at that point: the EU AI Act high-risk deadlines had just been deferred, the CIRCIA final rule was still pending, and Colorado's act was under a court stay. Confirm current status with counsel before acting on any row.

Ownership

Who owns which clock

Regulatory failures are usually ownership failures. Every regime needs a named owner for the determination (does this apply, did this event trigger it), a named owner for the notification, and a named owner for the ongoing obligations. These are rarely the same person.

Common pitfall

Building one process per regulation

Organizations that treat each regime as its own project end up with six incident procedures, four data inventories, and no idea which one is current.

What it looks like

The GDPR breach process lives with the DPO, the SEC materiality process with the general counsel, the NYDFS notification with the compliance team, and the HIPAA process with the privacy officer. An incident occurs. Four teams run four assessments in parallel with four timelines, none of them share a fact base, and the 72-hour clock expires while the materiality committee is being scheduled.

The counter-move

One incident process with one fact base and one timeline, with a regulatory overlay that maps the facts to each applicable clock. One control library mapped to every framework once, as in Governance, Risk & Compliance. One data inventory serving privacy, security, AI classification, and diligence. The regimes differ in their thresholds and their clocks, not in what they need to know.
