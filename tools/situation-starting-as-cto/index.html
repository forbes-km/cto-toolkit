Reading order

Where to start

Roughly sequenced: assessment first, then the relationships and structure that determine whether anything you decide sticks.

  1. Technology DiligenceEven without an acquisition, this is the lens for assessing what you've inherited.
  2. Application Portfolio ManagementBuild the TIME map of what's running before you propose changing any of it.
  3. Technology Decision RightsGet decision ownership explicit before ambiguity costs you in your first quarter.
  4. Working with the CEOThe relationship that determines whether anything else on this list works.
  5. Working with the CFOWhere the budget conversation gets litigated; build this relationship before you need it defended.
  6. Engineering CultureAssess what's rewarded, not what the values deck says, before you inherit a culture problem silently.
  7. CTO Leadership & SuccessionFind out on day one who runs things if you're out, not after you need the answer.
  8. The CTO Operating SystemSet your own cadence before you're consumed entirely by everyone else's.
  9. Technology Risk AppetiteState what you won't accept before you inherit someone else's silent tolerance for it.
  10. Board ReportingYour first board meeting will arrive faster than the 90-day mark suggests.
