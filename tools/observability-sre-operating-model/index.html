Observability & SRE Operating Model

Reliability as an Economic Decision answers how much reliability the business can afford. This page answers how the function that delivers it is organized, what it owns, and how to stand it up without a reorganization that changes titles and nothing else.

An SRE or reliability function that owns only incident response is an operations team. The list below is what distinguishes a reliability function from a rename.

Observability architecture that survives scale and cost

Observability platforms are now one of the largest line items in many cloud bills, and the cost usually grows faster than the value because nobody decided what to collect.

  1. Decide the signals and their purpose firstLogs for forensic detail, metrics for state over time and alerting, traces for request flow across services. A signal with no consumer is cost. Most estates over-collect logs and under-instrument traces.
  2. Standardize the instrumentation, not the vendorUse a vendor-neutral instrumentation standard (OpenTelemetry has become the default) so the collection layer is portable and the backend can be changed at a renewal. The backend is a SaaS renewal problem; the instrumentation should not be.
  3. Put a cost owner on telemetryIngest volume by service, with a budget per service and an alert when it is exceeded. Sampling for traces, retention tiers for logs, cardinality limits for metrics. The observability bill is a FinOps problem and belongs in Cloud FinOps.
  4. Alert on symptoms, not causesAlerts derive from SLOs: error rate, latency, availability as the user sees them. Cause-level signals (CPU, disk, queue depth) feed dashboards and the investigation, not the pager.
  5. Make the correlation identifier non-negotiableOne identifier propagates through every request, message, and log line. Without it, distributed debugging is guesswork, and the integration patterns in Integration & API Patterns depend on it.
  6. Include the AI layerModel calls, token usage, latency, cost, and output quality signals are telemetry too. An AI system in production without them is not observable; see AI Architecture.

On-call that people will keep doing

The failure mode of every reliability model is an on-call rota that burns out the people who know the most. It is a design problem, not a resilience problem.

  • Rotations are at least six people deep; fewer means someone is on call one week in four or worse
  • On-call is paid, either as an allowance or as time off, and the policy is written down
  • Pages per shift are measured; a shift with more than a handful of pages triggers a review of the alerts, not of the person
  • Every page has a runbook; a page without one is a bug filed against the service, not a test of the responder
  • Escalation is defined by service and by severity, with a named second line, so the first responder is never alone by default
  • Follow-the-sun is used where the organization spans time zones; if it does not, night pages are minimized by design, not endured
  • Toil discovered on call goes to a backlog with a budget, and the team sees it shrink
  • New engineers shadow before they respond, and nobody is on the rota for a service they have never deployed

Common pitfall

Renaming the ops team

The most common SRE adoption is a change of job title. It produces a team with the same reactive workload, a new set of expectations, and no authority to change either.

What it looks like

The operations team becomes the SRE team. Product teams are told they are now on call, escalate every page to SRE within minutes, and ship services with no SLO and no runbook because the checklist has no teeth. SRE is paged for everything, owns nothing, and attrits. Leadership concludes that SRE does not work here.

The counter-move

The function owns standards and the platform, with a production readiness gate that product teams must pass and an error budget policy signed by the executive who owns the roadmap. Teams that meet the standard run their own services; the reliability function's success measure is how rarely it is paged for something it does not own.
