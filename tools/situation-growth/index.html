Where to start

Get the architecture and org structure decisions right before scale makes them expensive to unwind.

  1. Enterprise ArchitectureThe capability model and operating structure that has to hold as headcount multiplies.
  2. Org DesignTeam topology and the span-of-control question that comes up in nearly every reorg conversation.
  3. Platform EngineeringSelf-service infrastructure and golden paths, before every new team reinvents its own.
  4. Technical Hiring & LevelingLeveling by scope and ambiguity, so hiring bar doesn't quietly erode as volume increases.
  5. Engineering MetricsDORA metrics and the distribution behind them, before the averages start hiding real problems.
  6. Security ProgramBuild it deliberately before scale forces a rushed retrofit.
