Cloud Service Mapper

A working reference for translating capabilities and services across AWS, Azure, GCP, and OCI, plus a side-by-side landing zone and foundational best-practices guide. Naming and feature parity shift as providers evolve their catalogs; treat this as a starting point for a conversation, not a guarantee of equivalence.

The building blocks every cloud foundation needs before workloads land on it: how accounts are structured, how identity and network are established, and how guardrails get enforced from day one. This is the layer landing zone automation (Control Tower, Landing Zones, blueprints, or a hand-rolled equivalent) is meant to stand up before a single application team gets access.

Reference only. Provider service catalogs and landing zone tooling change frequently; verify current naming, tiering, and feature parity directly against provider documentation before relying on this for a design or migration decision.
